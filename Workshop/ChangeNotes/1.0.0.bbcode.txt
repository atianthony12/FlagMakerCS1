[h1]Version 1.0.0[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]First release[/b]: cloth flags that wave in the game's wind, lit by its sun, shadows and street lights. Everything is in the in-game panel (F6).
[*][b]Any design[/b]: every country's flag, patterns in your colors, or your own pictures.
[*][b]Any size and shape[/b]: 0.5 to 30 m, seven shapes (swallowtail, pennant, burgee and more), hanging from the side or the top.
[*][b]Four motions[/b]: Waving (real cloth in the wind), Loop (a recorded wave played back at almost no cost, for hundreds of flags), Still or Flat.
[*][b]Wear and tear[/b]: worn, torn to tatters, dirty or holed flags (bullet holes, burns, tears), each its own.
[*][b]Flagpoles[/b]: optional, in four shapes with a ball, flat or spear top, a collar or cast base, halyard ropes, shine, rust and dirt, tilt and lean. The flag can fly lower on its pole (half-mast).
[*][b]Presets[/b]: Default, Wall flag (snaps to the wall under the pointer) or Banner (hangs from an arm on its pole).
[*][b]Keeps off the city[/b]: flags drape over roofs, walls, props and trees instead of passing through.
[*][b]Undo and redo[/b]: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y for everything you do to a flag.
[*][b]Quality[/b]: Low to Ultra in one setting. Crowds of flags share motion or loop, so the game stays smooth.
[*][b]Move It[/b]: move, copy and paste flags with their designs.
[*][b]Line Tool[/b]: rows of flags in a line, a curve or a circle, previewed before you click.
[*][b]Volumetric Weather[/b]: with it on, flags fly in its wind and gusts.
[*][b]Four languages[/b]: English, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese.
[/list]

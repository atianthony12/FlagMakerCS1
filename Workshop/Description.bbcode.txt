Fully animated, customizable flags that react to wind. Supports custom images (with cropping and adjusting), comes with all UN countries' flags, and a simple pattern creator.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] and this mod, and enable both (in Skyve too, if you use it).
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved). The mod does not run in the editors.
[*][b]Press F6[/b] (or click the mod's button) to open the panel. The key can be changed in the mod's options.
[*][b]Click + New flag[/b], choose a country flag, pattern or upload your own image.
[*][b]To edit a flag[/b], click Pick on the map, or choose it in the list on the Flags tab.
[/olist]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[h3]Flags[/h3]
[list]
[*]250+ countries flags (including EU, UN)
[*]Simple pattern creator
[*]Ability to upload your own flag picture
[*]Flags can be up to 30m wide/tall, hoisted from the side or the top
[*]Wear and tear, and dirt
[/list]
[h3]Flag motion[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Waving[/b]: the default, the flag waves in the wind and collides with other buildings
[*][b]Still[/b]: the flag animation will be paused. Useful for taking screenshots
[*][b]Flat[/b]: no motion at all, just a 2D flag
[*][b]Loop[/b]: will replay the flag animation over and over, so the flag no longer is affected by physics or wind. This is useful for taking videos, and saves performance if you have a lot of flags near each other and/or don't need them to react to the wind
[/list]
[h3]Flagpoles[/h3]
[list]
[*]Multiple base and top options, and it's optional so you can use existing poles or just attach them anywhere on your map
[*]Changeable colors, height and thickness
[*]Wear and tear, rust and dirt on the pole itself
[/list]
[h3]Performance[/h3]
By default the preset is set to High, you can set it to Low or Medium if your system struggles to animate the flags. You can also separately change the cloth quality itself.
[b]Technical info[/b]
The flags run on your CPU. By default it uses a minimal amount of cores but this is changeable in the Settings tab if you want a big amount of flags to animate at the same time. But you may never need to change this setting, because the mod employs a few ways to optimize performance, such as stopping flags animations except those near the camera, or having a shared motion/animation between flags, etc.
[h3]Mod options[/h3]
[list]
[*]4 panel themes
[*]Localization: English, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Tested with hundreds of mods, none of which conflicted.
[b]Removing the mod[/b] will not corrupt your save, but when you save without it the existing flags will be deleted permanently.
[h3]Works with[/h3]
[list]
[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1619685021]Move It[/url][/b]: the flags can be moved, rotated and deleted as Props.
[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2956939040]Line Tool[/url][/b]: flags can be added as a line, freeform, circle, etc. you will see this option in Flag Maker itself, not in the Line Tool toolbar.
[/list]
[h3]Known issues / limitations[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Flags can't turn to Procedural Objects[/b]: this is intentional for now, but if it becomes requested I will look into it.
[/list]

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder. On a Mac it is [i]~/Library/Logs/Unity/Player.log[/i], on Linux [i]~/.config/unity3d/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines/Player.log[/i].
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]FlagMaker.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Flag Maker) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
On a Mac it is in [i]~/Library/Application Support/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i], on Linux in [i]~/.local/share/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i].
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
Or [url=https://github.com/atianthony12/FlagMakerCS1/issues]open an issue on GitHub[/url] instead, where you can attach the log files directly.

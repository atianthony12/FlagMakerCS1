Fully animated, customizable flags that react to wind. Supports custom images (with cropping and adjusting), comes with all UN countries' flags, and a simple pattern creator.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] and this mod, and enable both (in Skyve too, if you use it).
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved). The mod does not run in the editors (read why below).
[*][b]Press F6[/b] (or click the mod's button) to open the panel. The key can be changed in the mod's options.
[*][b]Click + New flag[/b], choose a country flag, pattern or add your own image. The default motion (animation) is [b]Waving[/b], the flag reacts to physics and wind. Change it to [b]Loop[/b] if you want it to play the flag animation endlessly for better performance.
[*][b]To edit a flag[/b], click Pick on the map, or choose it in the list on the Flags tab.
[/olist]
[h3]Note on the Asset Editor[/h3]
It makes perfect sense for this mod to be available in the Asset Editor so you can add flags to your buildings. But, adding a flag to your asset will mean that you have to list this mod as a Required Item, which I'm not sure is best practice. I'm looking for a way to let you export flags as animations and share them without requiring this mod, but I'm not sure it's possible. If I give up on this, I'll make the mod work in the Asset Editor as well.

[h1]Overview[/h1]
[h3]Flags[/h3]
[list]
[*]250+ countries flags (including EU, UN)
[*]Simple pattern creator
[*]Ability to add your own flag picture
[*]Flags can be up to 30m wide/tall, hoisted from the side or the top
[*]Presets: With pole, Hanging on wall and Banner
[*]Wear and tear, and dirt
[*]Draggable as Props with [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1619685021]Move It[/url][/b], and can be placed in a line or circle with [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2956939040]Line Tool[/url][/b]
[/list]
[h3]Flag motion[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Waving[/b]: the default, physics-based, the most realistic, the flag waves in the wind and collides with other buildings. Recommended only to be used on a few flags at a time
[*][b]Loop[/b]: the flag will replay the waving animation over and over, and it's not affected by physics or wind. Best performance
[*][b]Still[/b]: the flag animation will be paused. Useful for taking screenshots
[*][b]Flat[/b]: no motion at all, just a flat 2D texture
[/list]
[h3]Flagpoles[/h3]
[list]
[*]Multiple base and top options, and it's optional so you can use existing poles or just attach them anywhere on your map
[*]Changeable colors, height and thickness
[*]Wear and tear, rust and dirt on the pole itself
[/list]
[h3]Performance[/h3]
By default the preset is set to High, you can set it to Low or Medium if your system struggles to animate the flags. You can also separately change the cloth quality itself.
The [i]Loop[/i] motion renders the flag animation on your CPU once and plays it back on your GPU, it's very cheap to animate, as opposed to [i]Waving[/i] which reacts to physics and wind.
With the [i]Waving[/i] motion, the flags run on your CPU, it uses a minimal amount of cores and it's changeable in the Settings tab if you want a big amount of flags to animate at the same time.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder. On a Mac it is [i]~/Library/Logs/Unity/Player.log[/i], on Linux [i]~/.config/unity3d/Colossal Order/Cities: Skylines/Player.log[/i].
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]FlagMaker.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Flag Maker) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
On a Mac it is in [i]~/Library/Application Support/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i], on Linux in [i]~/.local/share/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i].
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
Or [url=https://github.com/atianthony12/FlagMakerCS1/issues]open an issue on GitHub[/url] instead, where you can attach the log files directly.
